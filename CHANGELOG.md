# What's new

The newest version is first. Each version here matches a release on the [Releases page](https://github.com/heyshantanugupta/autosmm/releases).

## v1.2.11

- **Check for updates.** The Version tab in Settings has a Check for updates button, so you do not need to close the app. On Windows it can download and install right there.
- Fixed the green edge on the Today card looking doubled when you hover it.

## v1.2.10

- **Settings tabs.** Settings is now split into Model, Projects, UI Scale and Version. It opens on Model, and each button shows only its own section. The Projects list now sits open inside its tab.
- Light animations: Settings fades in and out, and tabs, buttons, menu items and plan rows react when you hover or press them. They use only simple fades and small moves, and switch off if your computer is set to reduce motion.
- The main card on Today widens its green edge and lifts when you hover it, and the number tiles and plan rows highlight too.

## v1.2.9

- **UI Scale.** Settings has a UI Scale choice (80% to 120%). The app now starts a little smaller at 90%.
- **Write tab.** After the next 7 days, "Show whole plan" lists every planned post, and the list switches to the whole plan on its own when nothing is due this week. "Write a different post" is now "Write my own post", and its page has "Back to my drafts".
- The side menu now stays at the left edge on full screen instead of floating in the middle.
- Settings now has a Projects section (switch, new, rename, duplicate, export, import, delete).

## v1.2.8

- A "Back to top" button appears in the corner once you scroll down a long page.

## v1.2.7

- **Read numbers from a screenshot.** On the Results page, choose a screenshot of a post and your AI fills in the likes, comments and views. You check them before adding. It works with Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude and Grok, and the screenshot goes to your AI provider like the rest of your brief.
- Results explains why there is no link field: most platforms keep post numbers behind a login, so reading a link would be unreliable, could break at any time and may go against the platform's rules.

## v1.2.6

- The version you have installed now shows at the bottom of Settings.

## v1.2.5

- **Easier form.** Every field now has a tag: Required, Recommended or Optional. A required field that is still empty shows a "!" until you fill it, and if you press Build with one missing, it is outlined so you can find it.
- **A short plain line under every field** says what to enter, with an example where it helps. "Proof" now explains itself: an article or review that mentions you, a number you can stand behind, or a customer quote you can use.
- Removed the grey example text from the facts box. Examples now sit in the line under the field.

## v1.2.4

- **Upload your logo.** Add it once in the Brand look section. It stays in this browser, one per project, and is never sent anywhere. Brand colors are filled in from it if you left them empty.
- **No logo yet?** The same section gives a ready prompt to make one in Gemini or ChatGPT, built from your product name, what it does, your audience, colors and style.
- **Add your logo and watermark to a picture.** On each post, after the image tool makes the picture, choose the file and download it back with your real logo and watermark in place. Image tools often misspell or redraw logos, so this step does it for them.

## v1.2.3

- **Update from inside the app (Windows).** When a new version is out, the banner has an Update now button. It downloads the update, then asks before closing AutoSMM to install it and open the new version. Your work stays saved. This starts with v1.2.3, so the update after it is the first one that installs itself.
- **Mac update popup.** Mac apps can only update themselves when signed by Apple, so on Mac a popup says a new version is out and links to the download. Open the file to install it over the old one. The portable Windows file does the same.
- Nothing downloads or installs unless you click.

## v1.2.2

- **Posts are written from your topic, not copied from it.** A title or short note is now treated as a brief. The writer works out the situation behind it and builds the post around a detail only your product has, instead of repeating your line and adding one. If a draft still opens with your topic, a warning says so.
- **Less generic writing.** Common filler phrases are banned, and every post needs a concrete scene and a product-specific detail. Strategy themes and 30-day plan topics are also tied to real audience situations and your facts.
- **Better image prompts.** Each post now comes with a detailed scene written for an image generator, in place of a one-line idea.
- **Brand look.** A new optional section holds your brand colors, picture style, logo note and watermark. Image prompts use the colors and style, keep clean space for your logo and watermark, and tell the tool not to draw them. You add your real logo file and text afterwards, so nothing is misspelled or redrawn.
- Start from your website picks up the site's theme color for your brand colors.

## v1.2.1

- **Better image prompts.** The prompt you paste into Gemini or ChatGPT is now laid out in clear lines: subject, composition for that platform, style, light and color, format, and what to avoid. A draft that only says "use a real screenshot" now becomes a scene that leaves room for your own screenshot.
- **Smaller and lighter.** The app's server files shrank from about 70 MB to about 23 MB, the installer carries English only, and the app's background process is capped in memory so older computers with little RAM cope better.
- **Update notice.** The desktop app now checks GitHub once a day and shows a small banner when a newer version is out, with a link to download it. It never installs anything by itself, and you can dismiss it.
- Start from your website now works on more sites: it retries the way a browser asks when a site refuses it, reads sites that build their text with JavaScript, and says plainly when a site blocks it.
- Build strategy no longer sits silently greyed out or shows a hidden tooltip. If the name or a fact is missing, it says what to add and jumps to that field.

## v1.2.0

- **Projects.** Keep a separate brief, strategy, plan, drafts and results for each product, like save slots. Use the project menu at the top to switch, start a new project, rename, duplicate or delete.
- **Export and import.** Save a project to a file as a backup or to move it to another computer, and load it again later. Files never contain your API key.
- Work saved by v1.1.x becomes your first project automatically.
- "Start a new project" on the Today screen no longer wipes your work.
- Fixed a crash when clearing the tone field on work saved by an older version.
- The checklist before posting now explains each point.
- Today shows the date and a welcome back line.
- Platform tags now use each platform's logo (LinkedIn, Instagram, X, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, Reddit).
- Spacing fixes: nothing sits against a border or looks squeezed, including the reply cards, Repurpose and Hook lab, Results, and the top bar on phones.
- The footer links to the maker's portfolio.
- **New colors.** A teal and green scheme replaces the blue and purple. Dark mode is a neutral dark grey. The logo is unchanged.

## v1.1.0

AutoSMM now has a home screen and four tabs: Today, Plan, Write, Reply and Results.

- **Today.** One next step, a short greeting, and your progress at a glance. If you are not sure what to do, start here.
- **30-day plan.** A balanced calendar of posts across your channels. Pick 3, 5 or 7 posts a week, download it as a CSV, and replan whenever you like.
- **Write the week.** One button writes your next 7 days of posts. Tick "I posted it" as you go.
- **Reply helper.** Paste a comment or message and get three replies in your voice. Complaints are flagged so you read them twice.
- **Negative reviews.** Calm public replies that do not argue, admit fault or promise refunds, and move the details to a private message.
- **Results and weekly review.** Log likes, comments and views, then get a plain review of what to do more and what to change. It tells you when there is too little data to be sure.
- **Your voice.** Paste 3 to 5 of your own past posts and drafts copy how you write. Facts in them are never reused.
- **Repurpose.** One idea or blog post becomes a LinkedIn post, an X thread, a Reel script and a carousel outline.
- **Hook lab.** Ten opening lines for any post.
- **Hashtags and keywords.** The right number of tags for each platform, plus search words.
- **Approval checklist.** Claims, link, tone and a read-aloud check on every draft, with a "Ready to post" sign.
- **Brief strength meter** that tells you the one thing to add next.
- **Start from your website.** Paste your address and AutoSMM fills in a first draft of your brief from what the page says. It only fills empty fields, drops anything the page does not support, and lists what the page did not say.
- **Light and dark mode button** in the top right corner. It starts from your computer's setting and remembers your choice.
- The TheInterviews.ai example brief is no longer part of the app.
- Cleaner README, and this changelog.
