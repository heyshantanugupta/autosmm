# What's new

The newest version is first. Each version here matches a release on the [Releases page](https://github.com/heyshantanugupta/autosmm/releases).

## v1.2.0

- **Projects.** Keep a separate brief, strategy, plan, drafts and results for each product, like save slots. Use the project menu at the top to switch, start a new project, rename, duplicate or delete.
- **Export and import.** Save a project to a file as a backup or to move it to another computer, and load it again later. Files never contain your API key.
- Work saved by v1.1.x becomes your first project automatically.
- "Start a new project" on the Today screen no longer wipes your work.
- Fixed a crash when clearing the tone field on work saved by an older version.
- The checklist before posting now explains each point.
- Today shows the date and a welcome back line.
- Platform tags now use each platform's logo (LinkedIn, Instagram, X, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, Reddit).
- Spacing fixes: nothing sits against a border or looks squeezed, including the reply cards, Repurpose and Hook lab, Results, and the top bar on phones.
- The footer links to the maker's portfolio.
- **New colors.** A teal and green scheme replaces the blue and purple. Dark mode is a neutral dark grey. The logo is unchanged.

## v1.1.0

AutoSMM now has a home screen and four tabs: Today, Plan, Write, Reply and Results.

- **Today.** One next step, a short greeting, and your progress at a glance. If you are not sure what to do, start here.
- **30-day plan.** A balanced calendar of posts across your channels. Pick 3, 5 or 7 posts a week, download it as a CSV, and replan whenever you like.
- **Write the week.** One button writes your next 7 days of posts. Tick "I posted it" as you go.
- **Reply helper.** Paste a comment or message and get three replies in your voice. Complaints are flagged so you read them twice.
- **Negative reviews.** Calm public replies that do not argue, admit fault or promise refunds, and move the details to a private message.
- **Results and weekly review.** Log likes, comments and views, then get a plain review of what to do more and what to change. It tells you when there is too little data to be sure.
- **Your voice.** Paste 3 to 5 of your own past posts and drafts copy how you write. Facts in them are never reused.
- **Repurpose.** One idea or blog post becomes a LinkedIn post, an X thread, a Reel script and a carousel outline.
- **Hook lab.** Ten opening lines for any post.
- **Hashtags and keywords.** The right number of tags for each platform, plus search words.
- **Approval checklist.** Claims, link, tone and a read-aloud check on every draft, with a "Ready to post" sign.
- **Brief strength meter** that tells you the one thing to add next.
- **Start from your website.** Paste your address and AutoSMM fills in a first draft of your brief from what the page says. It only fills empty fields, drops anything the page does not support, and lists what the page did not say.
- **Light and dark mode button** in the top right corner. It starts from your computer's setting and remembers your choice.
- The TheInterviews.ai example brief is no longer part of the app.
- Cleaner README, and this changelog.
